Players: 
- Multiplayer: 2–4
- Solo: 1 (vs AI)

Deck:
- Standard 52-card deck
- No jokers

Goal:
- Reach 31 points in one suit.

Card values:
- Ace = 11
- Face cards = 10
- Number cards = face value

Turn flow:
1. Initial bets.
2. Deal 3 cards face down to player(s).
3. Deal 3 cards face up into the "widow."
4. Player to the left of the dealer begins.
5. Player(s) may exchange 1 card from the widow with 1 card from their hand (face up.)
6. Repeat. If a player is content with the hand, knock.
7. Player with the highest score wins the pot.
